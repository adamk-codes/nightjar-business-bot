NIGHTJAR SKYWORKS — Business Summary

Nightjar Skyworks is a small Lebanese company that runs guided stargazing and astronomy experiences under some of the darkest skies in the Eastern Mediterranean. Founded in 2023, we operate a mountain basecamp in the Ehden highlands of North Lebanon (a Bortle 3 dark-sky site at 1,850 m) and a small booking office in Mar Mikhael, Beirut. Our mission is to reconnect people with the night sky, and to fight light pollution through education, so that the next generation in Lebanon still knows what the Milky Way looks like.

We offer six services. Starlight Walks are our signature 3-hour guided evenings combining naked-eye constellation tours with telescope viewing ($45 per adult, $25 under 16, Fridays and Saturdays, April to November). Astrophotography Workshops teach participants to photograph the Milky Way with their own camera ($160 per person, maximum 8 people, star-tracker rental $30 extra). School and University Field Nights bring curriculum-aligned astronomy to student groups ($18 per student, minimum 20 students, two teachers free). Private Dome Nights give a group of up to 6 people our observatory dome and an astronomer for the evening ($320 per group). Corporate and Event Skies are custom stargazing events for companies, weddings, and celebrations, priced by custom quote only. Finally, we rent beginner telescope kits for $35 per weekend with a $100 refundable deposit.

The company was founded by Dr. Rania Haddad, an astrophysicist who spent eight years studying variable stars before coming home to Lebanon, and Karim Nassar, a certified mountain guide who has led hikes on the Lebanon Mountain Trail for over a decade. They are joined by Lea Khoury, our astrophotographer and workshop lead, and Omar Saliba, who designs our school programs.

What makes us different: genuinely dark skies only two hours from Beirut, small groups (never more than 14 guests per guide), local astronomers who guide in Arabic, English, and French, and our Clear-Sky Guarantee: if the 2 pm forecast on the day of your booking shows more than 60% cloud cover, you can reschedule for free or get a full refund. A round-trip shuttle from Mar Mikhael is available for $15 per person.
